# Decision methods: input and inference contract

Use decision → method → required input → scales/thresholds → visual → allowed inference. Missing input is a disclosed gap, never an invented market, rating or weight. These are design and analytical guidance, not a promise that a connected MCP can render them.

## BCG: portfolio allocation
Define market, period, market growth and relative share with an explicit denominator/competitor and variant name. The classic relative-share split is 1; declare the growth benchmark instead of copying a screenshot's 100%. Sales alone cannot locate a business. Use bubbles whose AREA is proportional to a named nonnegative size measure, with a size legend; label reversed/log axes. Quadrants describe positioning, not proven profitability or an automatic divestment decision: supplement with economics. BCG's 2014 research uses a different competitor denominator and relative growth; do not silently mix that variant with the classic one. [BCG overview](https://www.bcg.com/about/overview/our-history/growth-share-matrix), [BCG revisited](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2014/growth-share-matrix-bcg-classics-revisited).

## ADL: lifecycle and competitive position
Use industry lifecycle embryonic/growth/mature/aging against competitive position weak/tenable/favorable/strong/dominant. Document placement evidence, date, definition of industry and uncertainty; company age is not industry maturity. With supplied evidence draw a labelled matrix showing positions and boundary uncertainty. The primary ADL article supports lifecycle/position concepts and category vocabulary, but the original full 20-cell prescription screenshot was not verified. Do not attribute automatic cell recommendations to ADL. [ADL Prism 1992](https://www.adlittle.com/sites/default/files/prism/1992_q4_06-12.pdf).

## Assessment matrices
Keep a custom competencies × development-potential rubric separate from the HR performance × potential nine-box and from GE–McKinsey industry attractiveness × competitive strength. Define each axis, ordered category thresholds, evidence, calibration process, assessment date and treatment of uncertain/boundary values. Labels carry matrix semantics independently of colors. Do not invent placements or make automated personnel decisions. [Korn Ferry talent review](https://www.kornferry.com/content/dam/kornferry/docs/article-migration/KornFerryTalentReview_12092014.pdf) supports calibrated performance/potential review; [McKinsey nine-box](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/enduring-ideas-the-ge-and-mckinsey-nine-box-matrix) describes portfolio strength/attractiveness.

## Multicriteria profiles and radar
Use comparable scales, fixed axis order/direction and explicit units/normalization. Preserve original units in accessible values; missing is not zero. Polygon AREA is not a ranking statistic. Keep overlays sparse; choose a heatmap, dot plot, small multiples or parallel coordinates for many entities or precise comparison. These are visualization safeguards; EU guidance specifically discusses axis-order dependence and the readability advantage of parallel coordinates over radar. [EU many dimensions](https://data.europa.eu/apps/data-visualisation-guide/visualising-many-dimensions).

## MCDA: trade-offs with uncertainty
Start with a performance matrix, defined criteria/scales and value functions mapping performance to scores. Obtain stakeholder-agreed swing weights over defined ranges. Additive aggregation requires mutual preferential independence and checks against double counting; otherwise reconsider criteria or the model. Never fabricate weights. An optional equal-weight scenario must be labelled as a scenario, not consensus. An interval score of 80 is not twice 40; score is not monetary benefit. Show sensitivity to weights, scores and uncertainty, and disclose an unstable winner. Treat costs according to the chosen appraisal method; the UK guide separates costs for its Green Book appraisal context, not as a universal prohibition on cost criteria. [UK Analysis Function MCDA guide](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/an-introductory-guide-to-mcda/).
