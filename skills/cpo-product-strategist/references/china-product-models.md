## 1. Evidence rule

Treat China as a heterogeneous and fast-changing market, not a collection of exotic tactics. Research current primary company disclosures, platform documentation, regulation, transaction data, and direct customer evidence. Distinguish national patterns from a specific city, platform, category, generation, or promotion period.

Platform-reported growth figures demonstrate that a mechanism operates at scale; they do not prove profitability, causality, transferability, or user preference outside the disclosed context.

## 2. Super-app and Mini Programs

Model a lightweight service embedded in a high-frequency host ecosystem. The product may acquire, identify, transact, pay, support, and reactivate without a separate app installation.

Measure:

- host-surface reach → Mini Program/service entry;
- entry → value activation → payment completion;
- time and steps to value;
- cross-surface conversion and repeat within the ecosystem;
- direct versus platform-mediated customer relationship;
- merchant integration cost, take/fees, data access, and dependency;
- retention and economics after platform incentives;
- failure exposure to platform policy or distribution changes.

Do not compare Mini Program MAU directly with standalone app MAU without identity, exposure, intent, and frequency definitions.

## 3. Public-to-private-domain loop

`Private domain` is an operating model in which a brand or merchant repeatedly reaches a consented customer through owned or directly managed relationships inside an ecosystem, such as groups, enterprise messaging, accounts, or Mini Programs.

Map:

```text
public discovery/content/offline code
→ consented relationship
→ first value or transaction
→ service/community interaction
→ repeat/cross-sell/referral
```

Measure consented activation, reachable audience, first-value conversion, repeat by cohort, share/referral-to-order, service burden, opt-out/block/complaint, incentive dependence, and contribution after operator labor. Group size, messages sent, followers, and coupon claims are not value outcomes.

## 4. Content-commerce-service loop

When content, recommendation, creator trust, transaction, payment, fulfillment, and support share one environment, diagnose the closed loop rather than separate media and store funnels.

Measure content-assisted qualified demand, view-to-value transition, creator/merchant economics, assisted conversion, refund/return and quality, repeat without promotion, cross-surface attribution uncertainty, and concentration in a platform or creator. Guard against engagement optimization that worsens product truth, trust, returns, or seller sustainability.

## 5. Instant retail and local services

Treat instant retail as a density and fulfillment system, not only a faster e-commerce funnel.

```text
Customer outcome
= eligible local demand
× discoverable available assortment
× fulfilled share
× on-time and correct delivery
× accepted quality
```

Track:

- assortment availability and inventory accuracy;
- promised versus actual delivery and tail latency;
- fill, substitution, cancellation, defect, refund, and repeat;
- orders, contribution, and variable cost by zone/time/category;
- demand, merchant, inventory, picker, and courier density;
- utilization, wait, income, safety, and churn for fulfillment participants;
- incentive dependence, peak capacity, weather and regulation;
- merchant contribution, concentration, and service quality.

Average delivery time can improve while tail failures, labor conditions, or economics worsen. Use actor and risk guardrails.

## 6. Transferability gate

Before importing a China pattern, test:

- comparable user habit and frequency;
- host-platform reach and permissions;
- payment, identity, logistics, and merchant infrastructure;
- privacy, labor, competition, and sector regulation;
- local density and service economics;
- customer acquisition alternative and platform dependency;
- organizational ability to operate communities or fulfillment.

Translate the mechanism, not the visible feature. A QR code, livestream, group, Mini Program, or 30-minute promise without the underlying distribution, trust, operations, and economics is not the model.
